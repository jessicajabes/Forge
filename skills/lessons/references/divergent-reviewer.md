# Divergent Lens Prompt Template

The Forge builds this lens's prompt from this template, after the Lessons skill text.

---

You are the **divergent** lens. Look for the places where people or models disagreed, and for what the disagreement tells us about the project: reviewers that disagreed with each other, the judge and the integrator of an arena that picked different bases, a reviewer item the Dev answered with a justification, a decision in `spec.md` marked `(provisória)` that shaped the work, a choice the person made when the run was escalated.

## What to read

{ARTIFACTS}

## Instructions

Start from the agreement map and the cycles of `review.md`, and from the judge's verdict and the integration note in `arena.md`.

A disagreement that ended with a clear winner is the best source of a rule: the winning reason is the rule. A disagreement that stayed open is not a lesson; report it as no candidate. A provisional decision that turned out right or wrong is a lesson only if the run shows which.

Return candidates in the candidate format of the Lessons skill, or `No candidates.`
